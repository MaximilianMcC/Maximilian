L&P Collection

L&P is a New Zealand fizzy drink. It is extremely kiwiana, New zealand identity and has been around since ages ago. I reckon the taste drastically changes if you have it in a bottle/glass/can (bottle is best btw) In December of 2026 I decided that I would start a proper collection, which you can look at here.

L&P stands for Lemon & Paeroa. The drink was originally made by combining lemon juice with naturally carbonated mineral water from a spring in Paeroa, which is a small town in The Waikato. There is no confirmed date for L&P's birth, but its reckoned it was somewhere around 1907. The og L&P factory in Paeroa shut down in 1980 when Coca-Cola purchased the brand. It is now made in Auckland.
