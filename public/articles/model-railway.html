Model railway

Ever since I first watched Thomas, I've wanted a model railway. I kinda instantly fell in love with how amazing the models used for the filming of Thomas were. I especially liked the style of the HiT era. Thomas from 2004-2008 Growing up I spent most of my time playing with TrackMaster: a range of motorised thomas toys. Over the years I amassed an incredibly large collection of it. Once every few months my parents would allow me to take over the lounge and dining room to make a layout that would pretty much take up the whole of the house. As I got a little older I began to start taking my layout-planning seriously. I would design actual prototypical layouts and I operated them very strictly. I know it is pretty cringe for a 19 year old to be glazing a bit of children's media, but I love Thomas so much and its something I'm no longer embarrassed to admit.

Here are some examples of the incredible TTTE sets. I found these all in like 10 minutes but if I do a rewatch I will definitely get higher quality screenshots. I could find a thousand more examples.
