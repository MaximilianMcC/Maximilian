The history of Vivids

A Vivid is a type of permanent marker produced by BIC. For over 40 decades Vivid has been a household name in New Zealand, so much to the point where it has become a generic trademark. Vivids still exist today, but their modern counterpart, in my opinion, is only a fraction as good as what they once were.

Why?

I was speaking to online friends, many American, about Sharpies. I asked if any of them had heard of Vivids, intending to ask which is more popular over there. None of them had heard of Vivid. I did a quick Google search to show them what I was speaking about, and found pretty much nothing. It was almost as if they never existed.

While doing this quick search, I learnt that Vivids were 'discontinued' years ago. I was still living my life thinking that tens of thousands of Kiwis were using the classic yellow and white marker. I would later find out that these have not been in production for over ten years.

This very same day I went to two different Whitcoulls, The Warehouse, and Warehouse Stationary. None sold the traditional Vivids that I was after. Instead, I found a new silver variation that had replaced the ones I remembered.
