Reading books

I am quite partial to reading. When I was little then I used to read a lot, but I stopped when I was around year five, so about nine-years-old. This was because I couldn't find anything that I was really interested in, and because I was too 'scared' to read stuff that wasn't for 'kids'.

In early 2025 I read Severance - The Lexington Letter, which is a very short ebook from the television series Severance. This got me back into reading. Before reading this then I had read an English translation of The Little Prince in 2024, but that was a one off thing that I only did because a friend requested me to. Prior to that, I am pretty sure that last time I read a book was in 2015 or something like that.

I was quite bad at reading when I was little. I used to go into special reading classes in like year three or something. Apparently I was quite apprehensive about learning to read because "reading is useless". I then found out that reading is not actually useless. Not learning stuff because its useless is still a thing I very much do today, and I am actually rather glad that I have held onto it. I feel as if it has saved me a lot of time.

This page shall have a collection of books that I have read since The Little Prince. The covers you see are the covers I own (I 100% judge books by their covers)
