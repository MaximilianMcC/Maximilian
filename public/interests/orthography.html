Orthography

Orthography is the set of rules that describes how a language is supposed to be written. I speak New Zealand English with a slightly stronger British influence than typical for a regular kiwi.

Most of my findings are naturally gathered by just talking to people from other parts of the world. Throughout this page you will find mini polls where you can contribute your country, and how you use the word/phrase which is being discussed.

I first developed an interest for orthography somewhere around 2024. During this time, I was speaking to a lot of my online friends. Often they would point out specific words that they had never heard of before, but were normal for me. This is when I realised that not all versions of English were the same. Around this time I also figured out that I had an accent, which started my interest in accents. I'll write an article about this one day. I love accents

"Heaps" was the first word that I remember learning to be nonstandard. I use this word a lot, so it came as a massive shock to me that no one else used it with the frequency that Kiwis and Aussies use it.
