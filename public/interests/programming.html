I've been interested in coding since about year 3-4. When I got to year five I wrote my first program. It was a game written in Bash. I remember googling "how to make a game" and clicking the first youtube link, which was a bash tutorial. This brought me down a Bash rabbit hole for years. Bash was very limiting, and it held me back a lot. I was always too scared to move on to a 'real' language though. I suppose it was kinda like imposter syndrome. In year 7 I finally moved on from Bash and started to learn how to make websites. I remember very clearly how I started: One day we were in the gym and I had forgotten my pe gear. Sir made me go on Education Perfect. I think I was supposed to do maths or something, but there was a html class and I did that. It was so fun and easy and stuff. I did the entire tutorial in the one pe lesson. Actually best pe class ever of all time ever. That one 20 minute html course was legit the only html learning that I ever did. After that, I followed this css course by EJ Media. I used notepad++ for a long time (had already been using it for bash) but soon moved to vscode (where I am writing this rn (I love vscode)) In year 9 I had a little go at Java, but it was pretty horrible. In year 10 I finally tried Python. I was introduced to it in dts (digital technology studies) at school. I used it for only a few weeks before giving Java a proper go. At the end of year 10 I fell in love with C#. There is no better language on this earth than C# god bless bill microsoft gates for blessing this world with such a tool. In 2025 when I started university I began to properly learn cpp.
