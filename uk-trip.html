The time I went to Europe

I went to England, Wales, and France in September/October of 2025. This was for railway 200 (celebrating the 200th anniversary of the modern railway) I have wanted to go to England since I was like four or something ngl. I never thought that I would go to France, but I went to see La La Land in concert. I used the channel tunnel to get there.
